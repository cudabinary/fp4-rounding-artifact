Subnormal p49 no-carry theorem with a complete reduced certificate
2026-10-21

Result and evidence level

Under the fixed contract below, every derived positive subnormal F32 scale
preserves the canonical b32 FP4 base and integer threshold at virtual RNE-p49.
The same holds for p50..64 by precision monotonicity. The known p48 witness
therefore makes 49 the sharp uniform precision within p35..64 on this subset.

The proof consists of algebraic reductions, a complete 4656-row coefficient/t
certificate, and the explicit exclusion of its single survivor. It does not
enumerate raw (amax,x) pairs. It is a computer-assisted theorem, not a pure
symbolic exclusion, a sample PASS, a proof-assistant development, or a native
GPU result. This replaces the old 100575-pair subnormal sufficiency dependency.
The complete full-domain p49 statement still uses the prior normal-scale
finite certificate (or its stronger p45 result). A compact symbolic normal
p45 proof remains open. No prior evidence file is changed.

1. Contract and canonical target

Inputs are finite nonnegative BF16 magnitudes 0<=x<=a. For a>0,
s=RN32(a/6), ties to even with gradual underflow. The zero-group fallback is
2^-42. Only scales with 0<s<2^-126 are considered in this theorem. Arbitrary
F32 inputs, independently selected scales, FTZ, NaNs and infinities are outside
this contract. Sign handling, RNG addressing and packing are unchanged outer
composition, not proved by this scalar argument.

Let G=(0,1/2,1,3/2,2,3,4,6). For y in [0,6), choose the unique k with
G[k]<=y<G[k+1] and define

 C(y)=(k, floor(2^32*(y-G[k])/(G[k+1]-G[k]))).

Set C(6)=(6,2^32). The target is equality of
C(min(RNE_p(x/s),6)) and C(min(x/s,6)), including the canonical base.
RNE-p is virtual binary arithmetic with binary64 exponent range, p35..64.
It does not imply that a native GPU p49 divide instruction exists.

2. All subnormal scales and all legal inputs

In units 2^-133, positive BF16 maxima producing subnormal s have exactly:

 a magnitude code    A range        q      a in those units
 1..255              1..255         15     A
 256..383            128..255       16     2A
 384..447            128..191       17     4A

At a code 448, a/6=2^-126 exactly. Monotonicity classifies the entire positive
subnormal-scale domain. Put D=s*2^149, using unnormalized F32 subnormal units.
Then D is the nearest integer to A*2^q/3, and

 3D=A*2^q+epsilon, epsilon in {-1,0,1}, 0<D<2^23.           (1)

There are no halfway cases. Epsilon is determined by A and q, not chosen
freely. Normalizing D to 24 bits would change epsilon; that is not done here.

Every positive x is X*2^v*2^-133, with these complete legality constraints:

 q15: v0, X1..A.
 q16: v0, X1..255; or v1, X128..A.
 q17: v0, X1..255; v1, X128..255; or v2, X128..A.

Consequently r=x/s=X*2^(16+v)/D. In particular r>2^-7 and r<8; nonzero
ratios and interpolation values avoid binary64 underflow/overflow here.
Zero is exact. If r>=6, monotonic rounding fixes 6 and clipping is exact.

3. Necessary carry conditions at p49

For 0<r<6, let S=33,32,31 for interval widths 1/2,1,2 respectively.
Set E=floor(log2(r)), L=16+v+S>=47, and N=X*2^L.
If N is divisible by D, r is already an exact threshold boundary. Otherwise
put j=D-(N mod D)>0, Q=ceil(N/D), so N+j=QD.

The next threshold boundary is Q*2^-S. Because r>2^-7, all binade endpoints
around r belong to this threshold lattice; hence its next boundary is at most
2^(E+1) and the binade immediately to its left is the binade of r. This makes
the half spacing immediately below that boundary exactly 2^(E-p), including
an upper power-of-two boundary. At p>=35, each relevant boundary is exactly
representable and even. Upward threshold crossing is therefore equivalent to

 j*2^J<=D, J=p-E-S.                                      (2)

Nearest rounding fixes each lower boundary and is monotone, so there is no
downward floor loss. Dyadic interpolation is exact (Sterbenz on nonzero
intervals, direct scaling at zero). An FP4 interval crossing also crosses a
threshold boundary. These are standard rounding arguments, not novelty claims.

At p49, E+S<=33, so J>=16 and j<=floor(D/65536). Equation (1) gives:

 q       global j cap       t cap below      fixed safe modulus exponent m
 15            42                 16                    15
 16            85                  4                    15
 17           127                  1                    13

In particular the j caps are 42,85,127, not the smaller p53 caps 2,5,7.
If epsilon=0, both D and N are divisible by 2^q. Then j is a positive
multiple of 2^q, contradicting j<=127. This branch is fully excluded.

4. Positive-integer reduction

For epsilon=+/-1, D is odd and gcd(A,D)=1. Since A*2^q is -epsilon modulo
D and L>=2q, let u=L-2q. Then

 t=(j*A^2+X*2^u)/D                                      (3)

is a positive integer, and

 3X*2^u=t*(A*2^q+epsilon)-3j*A^2,                        (4)
 Q=t*2^(2q)-3j*(A*2^q-epsilon).                          (5)

Here Q<=2^34. Combining (2) and (3) gives the strict bound

 0<t<2^(34-2q)+A^2*2^-J.                                (6)

Using J>=16 and the coefficient ranges yields t<=16,4,1 respectively.
The allowed v,S give u>=17,15,13 for q15,16,17. Reduction of (4) therefore
gives the necessary, deliberately weaker congruence

 3j*A^2 == epsilon*t (mod 2^m), m=15,15,13.               (7)

The generally valid modulus exponent is min(u,q). In particular q15 does
NOT permit dropping t*A*2^q modulo 2^u when u>q. The fixed moduli in (7)
are safe for every legal v,S, including candidates with the wrong interval.

5. Complete reduced certificate, not input samples

Enumerate only A and t in the displayed ranges: 255*16+128*4+64=4656
branches. The complete ledger records every branch in coefficient_ledger.csv.
For each epsilon=+/-1 branch put c=3A^2, M=2^m and g=gcd(c,M).
If g does not divide epsilon*t, (7) is impossible. Otherwise g<=t, and
c/g is invertible modulo M/g. Every solution j occupies one residue class
modulo M/g. Moreover

 M/g >= 2048,8192,8192 > 42,85,127, respectively.

Thus at most one j lies in 1..floor(D/65536). Checking the recorded residue
identity and its range proves completeness without searching j or raw pairs.
The verifier checks every expected A,t once, each residue equation, the
coprimality and uniqueness condition, and the applicable branch exclusion.
It does not assume the generator's modular inverse implementation is correct.

The sole survivor is

 q16, A230, D5024427, epsilon+1, t4, j51, m15.

It is an actual solution of the relaxed congruence, not a quantizer failure.
It shows why proving that (7) has no solutions would be the wrong obligation.

6. Exact exclusion of the sole relaxed survivor

For this survivor the right side of (4) is

 4*(230*65536+1)-3*51*230^2 = 52199424 = 3*531*2^15.

Every q16 legal input has u>=15. Equation (4) forces

 X=531*2^(15-u).

If u=15, X=531 exceeds the BF16 coefficient limit 255. If u>=16, X is
not an integer because 531 is odd. Hence no legal X exists, even before
the selected-interval and exact-binade guards. The generator retains all
six v,S reconstructions: one illegal X and five nonintegral X values.
There are no physical candidates. The exact guard J is not silently replaced
by 16 as a sufficiency test: using J>=16 only overapproximated failures, and
every element of that superset has now been excluded.

All possible p49 subnormal carries are excluded. Monotonicity of the crossing
condition extends the result through p64. The existing legal a242,x221
witness fails p35..48: j47 and D2643285 satisfy
47*2^15<=D<47*2^16. This supplies necessity without any new prefix sweep.

7. Status and remaining obligations

Proved here, with a complete small certificate: subnormal p49 no-carry and
its sharp p49..64 boundary within p35..64. The argument assumes only the
declared arithmetic contract and ordinary exact integer arithmetic. The
independent audit checks the case reduction and uses separate code; it is
not formal verification or a second physical experiment.

Full-domain p49 sufficiency is now the prior normal p45 finite result plus
this new subnormal result and zero/saturation composition. Its normal part
is inherited and remains certificate-assisted. R45 normal symbolic exclusion,
symbolic elimination of this 4656-row residue table, formal checking, the
universal b37 proof without old certificates, and closest-theory derivability
remain open. Rump Lemma 4.6 has not been obtained. Generic rounding and
integer tools are established prior theory; a failed direct theorem mapping
does not establish novelty. No speed, hardware, CUDA, or model-quality claim
follows from a virtual precision boundary.
