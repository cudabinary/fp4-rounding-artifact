2. Range premises can be stated explicitly

3. Staged interpolation is the canonical observation

same lower and upper grid endpoints as C(y), with the last [4,6] interval
also used at y=6. For a nonzero lower endpoint l and its upper endpoint h,
h<=2*l and l<=y<=h. Thus y-l is exact by Sterbenz; the l=0 subtraction
is exact directly. The endpoint difference h-l is one of1/2,1,2 and exact.
Dividing by that width and multiplying by2^32 are exact power-of-two scalings.
Their results have at most the working precision's significant bits and stay
in range: tiny zero-interval values remain far above binary64 underflow;
nonzero cancellation near a nonzero endpoint has quantum at least2^-64

The source's unsigned64 conversion therefore truncates this nonnegative value
