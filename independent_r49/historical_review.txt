Result

survivor and no legal BF16 reconstruction. No internal consistency failure

Coverage derived from the contract

In BF16 minimum units 2^-133, the three subnormal-scale classes are
A,2A,4A, with q15/A1..255, q16/A128..255, q17/A128..191. Exact division
by six and nearest F32-subnormal rounding give
  D=nearest(A*2^q/3), 3D=A*2^q+epsilon, epsilon in {-1,0,+1}.
The denominator-three fractions do not tie.

Every positive physical input is X*2^v minimum units. Legal v values are
0; 0,1; and 0,1,2 for the three classes. The full BF16 X bounds used in
reconstruction are:
  q15: v0 has X1..A;
  q16: v0 has X1..255, v1 has X128..A;
  q17: v0 has X1..255, v1 has X128..255, v2 has X128..A.

For a nonsaturated ratio, interval widths give S31/32/33 and E+S<=33.
At p49, J=49-E-S>=16, so a carry has 1<=j<=floor(D/65536).
The common t reduction requires epsilon=+/-1. For those scales D is odd,
gcd(A,D)=1, L=16+v+S>=47, and u=L-2q>=0. The relation
A*2^q=-epsilon modulo D implies A^2*2^(2q)=1 modulo D, giving the
positive integer t=(j*A^2+X*2^u)/D and
  3X*2^u=t*(A*2^q+epsilon)-3*j*A^2.
Reducing modulo 2^min(u,q) gives 3*j*A^2=epsilon*t modulo that power.

Using Q<=2^34 and the carry bound gives the strict upper bound
  0<t<2^(34-2q)+A^2/65536.
The program computes the largest integer strictly below this rational
separately for each A. It obtains maxima 16,4,1; none were hard-coded.
It also computes every min(u,q) over each class's legal v and S, obtaining
the safe fixed exponents 15,15,13. The resulting maximum j caps are
42,85,127. All these values are outputs of the declared derivations.

For epsilon=0, D and N are divisible by 2^q, so a carry j would have to
be a positive multiple of 2^q>=32768. The maximum j cap is only 127.
The program nevertheless records every relaxed A,t,j congruence for these
scales and separately checks this divisibility exclusion. Such rows do
not use the epsilon=+/-1 t lemma and are not coarse survivors.

Limits

This is analytic coverage plus a complete finite reduced-condition
certificate, not a nonenumerative symbolic proof or proof-assistant check.
No raw BF16 (a,x) input sweep, native/GPU execution, compiler/install,
network request, Git mutation, normal-scale enumeration, or performance
experiment was performed. Sufficiency depends on the stated scalar contract
and the mathematical coverage of the carry and t reductions. The work does
not establish publication novelty, unrestricted F32-scale safety, FTZ safety,
native quantizer semantics, or a normal-scale p45 theorem.
