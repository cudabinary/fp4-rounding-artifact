1. Refined Q and t bounds

Assume the normal-scale carry setup: r is positive and nonsaturated,
E=floor(log2 r), S is the selected threshold scaling exponent,
J=45-E-S, N+j=QD, Q=ceil(r*2^S), and the common t identity applies.
The generic grid bound E+S<=33 gives J>=12. Since D<2^24 and j>=1,
the carry bound j*2^J<=D gives J<=23.

By definition of E, r<2^(E+1). Thus
  r*2^S < 2^(E+1+S) = 2^(46-J).
For every original carry J12..23 this upper bound is an integer, so
  Q=ceil(r*2^S)<=2^(46-J).
From the exact common-lemma identity
  t=Q/2^(2q)+j*A^2/D-j/(D*2^(2q)),
the last term is strictly positive, and j/D<=2^-J. Consequently
  t < 2^(46-J-2q)+A^2*2^-J
    = (A^2+2^(46-2q))*2^-J.
No equality or ceiling case reverses the strict inequality.

For q18,A128..191, positivity of integer t requires
  2^J < 191^2+2^10 = 37505.
Since 32768<37505<65536, J<=15; together with J>=12 this leaves J12..15.
For q17,A192..255, the analogous requirement is
  2^J < 255^2+2^12 = 69121.
Since 65536<69121<131072, J<=16; this leaves J12..16.
The proposed refined coverage bounds are therefore sound.
