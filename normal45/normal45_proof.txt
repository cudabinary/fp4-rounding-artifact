Normal-scale p45 sufficiency: refined reduction and a small complete certificate
2026-10-21

Outcome

The fixed normal-scale predicate is true at p45..64 and false at p35..44.
This round provides an algebraic reduction to a complete small certificate:
1600 coefficient/t branches, 2814 surviving J/eta branches, and 236 residue
solutions. Of these, 234 reconstruct a coefficient outside [128,255]; the
other two contradict the exact eta condition. The prior 630848-representative
normal certificate is not needed by this new sufficient-condition proof.

The final residue exclusion remains computer-assisted. This is NOT a pure
symbolic p45 proof. The exact certificate-free arithmetic lemma is stated in
section 8. There is no new general rounding theorem or publication-novelty
claim. The independent R49 work is a separate result and evidence stream.

1. Fixed predicate and assumptions

Let B contain nonnegative finite BF16 magnitudes. Inputs satisfy 0<=x<=a.
For a>0 set s=RN32(a/6), ties to even with gradual underflow. This theorem
requires s to be normal and positive. A zero maximum uses the unchanged
fallback 2^-42 and is exact separately; positive subnormal scales belong to
R49, not to this normal p45 theorem.

Use G=(0,1/2,1,3/2,2,3,4,6). For y in [0,6), select the unique k with
G[k]<=y<G[k+1] and set
 C(y)=(k,floor(2^32*(y-G[k])/(G[k+1]-G[k]))).
At y=6 use C(6)=(6,2^32). P_N(p) means
 C(min(RNE_p(x/s),6))=C(min(x/s,6))
for every legal normal-scale pair. The b32 lattice is fixed. Virtual RNE-p
uses binary64 exponent range, with integer 35<=p<=64; no working underflow
or overflow occurs in this domain. FTZ, arbitrary F32 inputs, arbitrary
scales, a changed RNG word contract, CUDA execution and native p45 hardware
are not included. Sign and packing remain unchanged outer composition.

2. Normal scale and normalized input identities

Write a=A*2^(ea-7), 128<=A<=255. Normal RN32(a/6) has integer 24-bit
significand D, 2^23<=D<2^24, and
 3D=A*2^q+epsilon, epsilon in {-1,0,1},
 q18 for A128..191; q17 for A192..255.                    (1)
Epsilon is determined by nearest integer rounding of A*2^q/3; no tie exists.
The scale is s=D*2^(ea-q-8), so x/s=X*2^(d+q+1)/D when
x=X*2^(ex-7), X128..255, and d=ex-ea. Normalized BF16 subnormal x has
trailing zeros in X and is included by this representation. Legality gives
d<=0 and X<=A when d=0. Relaxing these two constraints below only enlarges
the hypothetical-failure set, so cannot exclude a legal failure incorrectly.

Since X*2^16/D is in [1/2,2), put eta=1[X*2^16<D]. Then, exactly,
 E=floor(log2(x/s))=d+q-15-eta.
For an interval width 2^(32-S), S in {31,32,33}, let
 L=d+q+1+S, N=X*2^L.
Whenever N is an integer, its exact exponent relationship is
 E+S=L-16-eta; at p45, L+J=61+eta, J=45-E-S.              (2)
Eta is an input-dependent binade condition, not a free bit.

3. Crossing, including the tiny-ratio case

Zero and r=x/s>=6 are exact after clipping. For 0<r<6, choose its exact
interval and S. If r*2^S<1/2, monotonic RNE fixes the exactly representable
half-threshold value 2^(-S-1), so the rounded ratio stays below 2^-S.
The interval is the zero interval and both canonical thresholds are zero.
This handles tiny ratios before using r's exponent in a midpoint argument.

For r*2^S>=1/2, the next threshold B=ceil(r*2^S)*2^-S has left-hand binade
E, including B at an upper power-of-two boundary. For p>=35 it is an even
representable number. When L<0, X*2^L/D<2^-16, already in the tiny case.
Otherwise N is integral. If D divides N, the ratio is a threshold and exact.
For a positive remainder put j=D-(N mod D), Q=ceil(N/D), N+j=QD.
Half-ULP separation and even tie selection give
 upward crossing iff j*2^J<=D.                           (3)
Lower thresholds are fixed and nearest rounding is monotone, so downward
floor loss is impossible. Exact dyadic interpolation (Sterbenz for nonzero
intervals) and threshold coverage of FP4 endpoints preserve the canonical
base as well. These are standard rounding facts.

The grid gives E+S<=33, hence J>=12 at p45. A carry with j>=1 and D<2^24
requires J<=23. Thus L>=38 and L>=2q, including q18. If epsilon=0, D and N
are divisible by 2^q, so positive j is a multiple of 2^q. Equation (3)
instead gives j<=4095<2^q, a contradiction. Only epsilon=+/-1 remains.

4. Positive-integer transform and a stronger bound

D is odd and gcd(A,D)=1. Let u=L-2q>=0. From (1),
A^2*2^(2q)=1 modulo D. Therefore
 t=(j*A^2+X*2^u)/D
is a positive integer, with exact identities
 3X*2^u=t*(A*2^q+epsilon)-3j*A^2,                        (4)
 Q=t*2^(2q)-3j*(A*2^q-epsilon).                          (5)
Also
 t=Q/2^(2q)+j*A^2/D-j/(D*2^(2q)).

The sharper observation in this round is Q<=2^(E+S+1)=2^(46-J).
Combining it with (3) and the strictly negative last term gives
 0<t<(A^2+2^(46-2q))*2^-J.                               (6)
This retains the dependence between Q, J and the ratio binade.

For q18, A^2+1024<=37505<2^16; thus J12..15 and t<=9.
For q17, A^2+4096<=69121<2^17; thus J12..16 and t<=16.
Equations (2) now give u10..14 for q18, u11..16 for q17. In particular u<=q.
Reducing (4) modulo 2^u is therefore valid:
 3j*A^2=epsilon*t modulo 2^u.                            (7)
Unlike subnormal q15, no discarded t*A*2^q term survives at this modulus.

5. Complete coefficient certificate and its checker

The A,t superset has 64*9+64*16=1600 branches. Exclude epsilon-zero branches
by divisibility as above. Retain only J12..23 obeying strict bound (6), and
both eta values. These are exactly the 2814 rows in
normal45_branch_certificate.csv. The strict bound itself implies the tighter
J ranges above; the checker does not assume them to skip any branch.

For c=3A^2, M=2^u, g=gcd(c,M), a solution requires g|epsilon*t. If this
fails, the row records a gcd contradiction. Otherwise c/g is invertible
modulo M/g, so all j are one residue class. The certificate supplies that
residue. normal45_certificate_verifier.py checks the residue equation,
coprimality, its canonical range, and EVERY positive member through
floor(D/2^J). It does not perform modular inversion. Every such j must appear
exactly once in normal45_reduction_results.json, with X from (4).

There are 1290 gcd contradictions among the 2814 rows. The remaining rows
produce 236 j solutions; some contain no j in the carry range. Of the 236,
234 give X outside128..255. The two inside the range are displayed below.
Complete branch coverage plus full residue-class accounting is the universal
argument, not sampling. No raw (amax,x) pairs or old representatives are run.

 q   A    D          eps  t  J   eta  L   u    j     X
 18  142  12408149   -1   4  12   1  50  14  2277  227
 17  239  10442069   -1   5  12   1  50  16   649  231

Both are excluded algebraically by eta's definition:
 227*65536=14876672 >12408149,
 231*65536=15138816 >10442069.
Their actual eta is zero, contrary to the eta=1 branch that produced them.
Thus there is no legal normalized input, before needing x<=a or exact S.
Dropping eta consistency would leave two false counterexample candidates.

All p45 crossings have been excluded. Equation (3) and representability
extend sufficiency to p46..64. This is a complete computer-assisted proof
of P_N(p) for p45..64, independent of the old normal raw-pair certificate.

8. Exact remaining certificate-free lemma

For q,A,D,epsilon fixed by (1), t positive and J,eta satisfying
 J12..23, eta in {0,1}, t*2^J<A^2+2^(46-2q),
 L=61+eta-J, u=L-2q,
there are no integers j,X with
 1<=j<=floor(D/2^J), 128<=X<=255,
 3X*2^u=t*(A*2^q+epsilon)-3j*A^2,
 eta=1[X*2^16<D].

This lemma is TRUE by the complete finite certificate in section 5. What
remains OPEN is a short symbolic derivation replacing that coefficient
enumeration. The proof target is not the false claim that the relaxed
congruence has no solutions. Any such proof must retain eta or an equivalent
physical binade guard. No symbolic proof is asserted by relabeling this table.
